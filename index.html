Social Media Use and Focus Difficulty

Statistical Modeling and Simulation · Team of 5 · 2026

Which social media habits go with trouble focusing? Using survey responses from 371 young adults, we mined association rules and compared groups with ANOVA and chi-square tests. Restlessness and purposeless use were more strongly linked to focus difficulty than time spent alone. I cleaned the data and wrote the abstract, limitations, and references.

Catbot: Reinforcement Learning Agent

Intelligent Systems · Group project · 2025

A bot that learns to catch cats with different movement behaviors on an 8×8 grid. It uses tabular Q-learning, a reward function shaped around the distance to the cat, and an exploration rate that decays linearly to zero over the first 1,000 of 5,000 training episodes. This is the project that got me curious about how a computer learns.

PinoyBot: Filipino-English Word Identifier

Intelligent Systems · Group project · 2025

Labels each word in a Filipino-English sentence as Filipino, English, or other. A Naive Bayes classifier using seven hand-built word features and 742 annotated words reached about 88% test accuracy, though it is weaker on English words. I built the feature and labeling pipeline and connected the model to the tagging function.
